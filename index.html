Choose a product to place, inspect, customize, and add to your cart in augmented reality.

Spatial Shopping is an open-source WebXR demo built with Meta's Immersive Web SDK (IWSDK). Products, prices, and the cart are sample data; nothing is sold.

AR works in Meta Quest Browser on Meta Quest 3, 3S, and Pro.
